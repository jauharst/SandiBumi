Step by step: a rock-type ladder you can defend

The simplest rock typing there is: a declared Vsh + PHIE cutoff ladder, three classes, best to non-net. The ladder ships absent because a cutoff is an interpretation, not a constant. And the module's whole design assumes you will validate the ladder against a core-derived rock type with the confusion-matrix QC before attaching per-class laws to it.

Deriving a starting ladder from the data itself

Rather than typing folklore numbers, this walkthrough reads the ladder off the dataset's own sand population (samples at GR < 60):

  • VSH1 0.458 / VSH2 0.467, the P90 and P95 of VSH within the sands: the sand population's own upper edge, and a slightly forgiving variant of it.
  • PHI1 0.148 / PHI2 0.134, the P50 and P25 of PHIE_DEN within the sands: median sand quality, and the lower quartile.
  • The pane enforces VSH1 ≤ VSH2 and PHI1 ≥ PHI2, so a ladder that ranks backwards is refused before it classifies anything.

A percentile ladder is a starting point with a defence (every number has a command behind it), not a validated rock type. The validation is the tie-in.

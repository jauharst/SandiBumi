Step by step: a synthetic log by nearest neighbours

Synthetic Log trains on this run's own samples — wherever the target and every predictor are present — and predicts the target everywhere the predictors exist, by distance-weighted K-nearest-neighbour regression in z-scored predictor space. No model file, no training session: the well is its own training set.

The picks

  • Target RHOB from GR / NPHI / DT, K 10 — the shipped defaults.
  • OPT_COMBINE MAX_RAW — the washout rule: bad hole only pushes RHOB down, so taking max(raw, synthetic) repairs washed-out density while leaving every good sample exactly as logged.
  • Mask BADHOLE — the rule the pane documentation states: washed-out samples must never train the model, or the synthetic learns the washout it exists to repair.

Run and read

On these wells: 3 clean, and MAX_RAW raised 567 samples above the delivered density — the washout intervals, where the logged RHOB is mud-biased low and the synthetic (trained on good hole) reads the rock instead. RHOB_SYN beside RHOB in a track shows the repair exactly where the caliper says it should be, and nowhere else.

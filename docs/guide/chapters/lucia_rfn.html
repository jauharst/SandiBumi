Step by step: the Lucia rock-fabric number

Carbonate rock typing: the Jennings & Lucia global porosity-permeability transform is inverted analytically for the rock-fabric number, which is then binned into the three Lucia classes (grainstone to mud-dominated). Two things the pane's documentation asks you to respect: the porosity should be interparticle porosity (subtract separate-vug porosity where you have it), and on a clastic-dominated field this module is for the carbonate stringers, not the sands.

The run

No parameters: the global transform's constants ship as transcribed from the paper, with the documentation's own instruction to verify first. Inputs are PHIE_DEN and PERM_XFM selected by name (see the rock-typing chapter for why the generic PERM slot is never the right choice).

Run and read

On these wells: 3 clean, and only 49% of samples classified, which is the module being honest rather than incomplete: RT_LUCIA is written only where the computed RFN lands inside the 0.5–4 band the classes are defined over, and a clastic section computed through a carbonate transform spends half its samples outside it. On a real carbonate, or a stringer within this section, the classified fraction is the first QC number to read: a low fraction means the transform and the rock disagree about what family the samples belong to.

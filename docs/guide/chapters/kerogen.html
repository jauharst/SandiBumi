Step by step: from TOC weight to kerogen volume

TOC is a weight percent; kerogen occupies a volume disproportionate to its weight because it is light — and that volume inflates the density log's apparent porosity. This module makes both corrections explicit: TOM converts TOC to an organic-matter weight fraction (the K_TOC2OM factor covers the H/O/N/S the carbon measurement misses), VKER converts that to a volume fraction of the bulk rock, and PHIT_OMC removes it from total porosity.

The picks

TOC from the Passey run; RHO_KERO 1.10 g/cc and K_TOC2OM 1.2 as shipped. The 1.10 kerogen density is deliberate: it matches the SandiMin Kerogen mineral, so VKER is directly comparable to a SandiMin VOL_KEROGEN solve. Another vendor tradition seeds 1.25 — override if your workflow follows it, but keep one convention per study.

Run and read

On these wells: 3 clean. VKER's median is 0 — most of this section carries no computed TOC, as the Passey chapter explains — with a maximum of 0.045 v/v where the gas-zone false-positive TOC leaks through. That chain is worth noticing: a spurious TOC becomes a spurious kerogen volume becomes a porosity correction, which is why the Passey chapter's reservoir-interval caution matters two modules downstream.

SW — Indonesia (Poupon-Leveaux)

Module id sw_indo · category Saturation

The Poupon-Leveaux Indonesia equation for shaly sands: total conductivity is a clay term, a clean-sand Archie term and a geometric cross-term between them, so the clay contribution needs no separate clay-water resistivity model. Three published forms of the shale exponent are offered by name.

1/RT = (v/RT_SH + PHIE^M/(A·Rw) + 2·√(v·PHIE^M/(A·Rw·RT_SH))) · SW^N
v = VSH^(2−VSH)  (FULL),  VSH²  (SIMPLE),  VSH^(2−2·VSH)  (TAR_SAND)

References

  • Poupon, A. & Leveaux, J. (1971), SPWLA 12th Annual Logging Symposium, Paper O

Step by step: the Indonesia equation

Poupon-Leveaux's Indonesia equation writes total conductivity as a clay term, a clean-sand Archie term and a geometric cross-term between them, built for shaly sands where a separate clay-water model is more parameters than the data can support. The pane offers the three published shale-exponent forms by name; this run uses FULL (v = VSH^(2−VSH)), the default.

The picks

Same convention and water pick as the Archie chapter, plus the one parameter Indonesia adds, the shale resistivity:

  • A 1 · M 2 · N 2: the dossier's reference-case convention, declared.
  • RW 0.058 ohm·m: median wet-sand Rwa, as before.
  • RT_SH 2.5 ohm·m: the median RES_DEEP over the 55 samples at GR ≥ 112 gAPI, this dataset's own shale resistivity.
  • SWE_IRR 0.16: the SSC-derived irreducible floor, as before.
  • VSH input: the VSH curve from the gamma-ray run; PHIE pinned to PHIE_DEN so the porosity's provenance is one method, not a mixture.

Run and read

On these wells: 0 clean · 3 degraded, and the report is worth reading closely. The clamp count drops to ~93 per well (against Archie's 372) because the clay term now absorbs the shale conductivity that Archie could only explain as water. One sample per well reports "VSH ≥ 1 leaves no effective porosity, so the saturation is degenerate rather than measured", the equation refusing to manufacture a number where the rock model says there is no reservoir.

The comparison that teaches the method: in the clean gas sand the median SWE_INDO is 0.084, identical to Archie's, because at VSH → 0 the Indonesia equation is Archie. The two separate only where VSH grows, which is exactly the interval a shaly-sand equation exists for. Put SWE_ARCH and SWE_INDO on one track and the divergence maps the shale.

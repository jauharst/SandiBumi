TOC — Passey ΔlogR + Schmoker

Module id toc_passey · category Unconventional

Total organic carbon from the Passey ΔlogR overlay - the separation between deep resistivity and a baselined porosity curve, converted to TOC with a maturity term - plus the Schmoker-Hester density TOC as an independent cross-check whenever a density log is present.

sonic overlay: ΔlogR = log10(R/R_BASE) + 0.02·(DT − DT_BASE)
density overlay: ΔlogR = log10(R/R_BASE) − 2.5·(RHOB − RHOB_BASE)
TOC = ΔlogR·10^(2.297 − 0.1688·LOM) + TOC_BG  [wt%; ΔlogR < 0 floors to the background]
cross-check: TOC_SCHMOKER = 154.497/RHOB − 57.261

References

  • Passey, Creaney, Kulla, Moretti & Stroud (1990), "A practical model for organic richness from porosity and resistivity logs," AAPG Bulletin 74(12): 1777-1794
  • Schmoker & Hester (1983), "Organic carbon in Bakken Formation," AAPG Bulletin 67(12): 2165-2174

Baselines are picked on a non-source, clay-rich interval where the two curves overlie, and TOC is very sensitive to them. LOM 6-12 (Hood-Gutjahr-Heacock scale); the conversion is calibrated to LOM <= 12. The Schmoker fit is Bakken-specific - a cross-check, not a substitute.

Step by step: TOC from the ΔlogR overlay

Passey's ΔlogR reads organic richness from the separation between deep resistivity and a baselined porosity curve — sonic here — converted to TOC with the maturity term 10^(2.297−0.1688·LOM). A Schmoker-Hester density TOC rides along as an independent cross-check whenever RHOB is present.

First, the refusal

"R_BASE must be set — Baseline resistivity (non-source interval)"

Baselines ship absent because TOC is very sensitive to them, and a baseline belongs to your section, not to a default.

The picks, each with its defence

  • R_BASE 2.5 ohmm / DT_BASE 117.9 µs/ft — the medians of RES_DEEP and DT over this dataset's shale (GR ≥ 112): the clay-rich, non-source interval where the two curves overlie, which is exactly where the method says to pick them.
  • LOM 10.6 — the recorded vendor seed (a Tier-A, per-well-overridable starting value in this project's method record). On a real study LOM comes from measured Ro or Tmax, or is iterated to match core TOC.
  • TOC_BG 0 — a barren baseline rock.

Run and read: a non-source section says so

On these wells: 3 clean — and honest numbers. ΔlogR is negative at 574 of 590 samples: a water-and-gas-bearing clastic section is not a source rock, and the overlay says exactly that (TOC floors to the background). The 16 positive samples sit in the gas sand — hydrocarbon resistivity, not organics, faking a small separation: the classic gas-zone false positive Passey's own paper warns about, worth knowing before quoting any TOC from a reservoir interval. And the Schmoker cross-check reads a median 7.4 wt% here — absurd on a 2.3 g/cc clastic, because that fit is Bakken-specific: it is a cross-check on a real source-rock section, never a substitute.
